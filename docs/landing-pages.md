# Work and edition landing-page presentation

Press's permanent work and numbered-edition pages are **bibliographic covers**
for the issued reading copies, not a second edition or a DOI registration service.
Their data comes exclusively from reviewed provenance, publication receipts,
notices and the existing work/edition identifier registrations. The page's HTML
and inline stylesheet are generated by `scripts/public_record.py` and retained
as approved public-record bytes. No new metadata field, registration rule, or
runtime dependency is introduced by this presentation.

## Visual references and deliberate choices

- [Zenodo](https://help.zenodo.org/docs/deposit/manage-versions/):
  distinguish a DOI for the *work across editions* from one for a *specific
  version*, with direct access to the exact artifact. Reject automatic
  latest-version resolution for Press concept DOIs; the work page presents all
  edition records and their current statuses.
- [arXiv abstract pages](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.00001): lead with an actual
  publication title, authors, concise publication facts, and an obvious reading
  link. Reject discovery widgets, download statistics, promotional panels, and
  PDF-first conventions: Press issues verifiable standalone HTML.
- [W3C technical reports](https://www.w3.org/TR/): make version and publication
  status legible. Preserve an explicitly withdrawn or superseded edition and its
  notice history without making it look current. Reject additional status
  taxonomies, process badges, or a new rule for which edition is authoritative.
- [GOV.UK Summary List](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/summary-list/):
  use clearly labeled, aligned, wrap-friendly facts for provenance. Reject a
  permanent metadata sidebar that would crowd phones or wide identifiers.
- [DataCite landing-page guidance](https://support.datacite.org/docs/landing-pages):
  show the **complete clickable DOI URL** and identify what the DOI resolves to.
  Reject new keywords, affiliation, citation-export, or registry-specific fields.

The typography, paper/ink colors, link colors, dark mode, print rules, and
focus indicators inherit Press's existing reading-surface decisions. These
pages deliberately use only static inline CSS, semantic HTML, and system fonts.

## Reading order and meaning

The **work page** leads with the selected, reviewed work title and ordered
attribution (from the existing `citation_edition` policy where explicitly set,
otherwise the deterministic initial basis). It identifies the work across
editions and shows the permanent URL and current concept DOI *if assigned*.
The complete editions list follows, newest version first, preserving each
edition's actual title, date, status and replacement links. The work-level
citation keeps its original first-publication year and currently assigned
concept DOI; an edition DOI is never used to identify the work. DOI history
remains available below the citation.

The **edition page** leads with its immutable title, ordered authorship,
numbered version, actual publication date, current status and optional edition
DOI. Notices and explicit successor warnings come **before** the reading link,
so a withdrawn document is never visually presented as current. The link opens
the exact archived reading copy even when the edition is withdrawn. Optional
abstract text, exact-edition citation, labeled edition details, other existing
identifiers and initial editorial notes remain present. The citation's
publisher, authors, title, DOI and publication date come from the same reviewed
facts as before; the design adds no new metadata sources or hidden JSON-LD.

The DOI link text is the full `https://doi.org/...` address rather than just the
bare identifier. Long identifiers, source hashes and work URLs wrap without
truncation or horizontal page overflow. The paper layout remains linear; no
cards, tooltips, scripts, custom fonts, metadata sidebar, or color-only status
signal are introduced.

## Review before the first DOI registration

The repository's `make preview` builds a *simulated*, non-authoritative site
with the same public-record generation functions. Consult `make help` for its
arguments; the issuer must still verify a real staged edition and its official
receipts before announcement. During integration, inspect work and edition
pages at 320, 375, 768 and desktop widths, with system dark/light preferences,
keyboard focus, enlarged text, and printed white paper under a dark preference.
Include cases with no DOI, no abstract, multiple authors, mixed-language
editions, supersession/withdrawal, corrected concept DOI, and long provenance.

Verify that the record generator, work-citation selection from #45, source/asset
checks, independent recovery checks and byte-identical previously issued
reading copies all continue to pass. Existing public records may be
regenerated through the normal review and checkpoint process; the **issued
reading artifact is never re-styled in place**. Registration and DOI resolver
behavior are not exercised merely by visual preview.
